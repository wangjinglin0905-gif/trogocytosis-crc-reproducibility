# Table S1. Evidence annotation and screen coverage of the 39-gene panel

References document the evidence supporting each annotation; where original selection citations were unavailable, contextual references were added during evidence annotation. Historical literature chains and subsequently added contextual citations are distinguished in the source_scope field of the accompanying CSV. In particular, the CD24 and SIGLEC10 references support their historical phagocytic-checkpoint grouping but are not presented as recovered original selection citations. This annotation does not reconstruct a complete contemporaneous selection record. Protein-transfer reports do not validate recipient-cell RNA as an event measurement. ANO6 is supported here by an explicitly labelled preprint, without asserting that no later journal version exists.
